# Proposed research categories — all 85 technology areas

Extracted from the bundled original Data/TechArea.txt, including racial and special-unlock areas. Every area appears once below. Grouping is a proposal; no research dependencies, costs, levels or access rules are changed. Within each category, original data order is preserved.

I suggest eight primary categories. Science contains foundational and racial research; racial weapons still live under Weapons, with their racial access shown. Optional cross-tags can surface them from a racial overview without duplicating the underlying technology. Units and mobile infrastructure share a branch because they are design/build unlocks. Point-defense is placed under Defense because its purpose is interception. Gravitational Technology is a special stellar-engineering unlock, while Gravitational Weapons remain under Weapons.

“Every tech” here means every research area, not every numbered level. Maximum levels are shown so the complete area coverage is explicit. Exact names retain the game data spelling.

- Before consolidating numbered upgrades, audit concrete unlocks and numerical improvements at each level. Any streamlined ruleset should be a separate new-game option with explicit AI and balance testing.
